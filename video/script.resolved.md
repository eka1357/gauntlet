## 04 Before and after

Under the realistic prompt, 49 of 190 standard attacks led to an unsafe action before hardening. Gauntlet clustered the failures and wrote Policy v2. Under Policy v2, 0 of 190, with a 95% upper bound of 2.0 percent.

## 04b Live run

This is a live run, recorded locally. Fresh email attacks are generated on the spot and run against the same agent, with and without Policy v2. Short runs vary, so the recorded run carries the evidence.

## 05 Still does its job

A fix is useless if the agent stops working. On 35 benign tasks, repeated 3 times, the agent passed 100 of 105 before hardening and 100 of 105 after. A deny-everything control passed 6 of 35, so the suite does catch a broken agent.
